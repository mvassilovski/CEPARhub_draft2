Directory

Nobody looks for a collaborator alphabetically

Search by what the work is about, or open the filters to narrow by topic, method, community, place, discipline or school. Select anyone to see their full profile.

Today this matches keywords across every field on a record, including what people say they want to work on next, which is often where a collaboration actually starts. The structure is built so a natural-language version can replace it later without re-tagging anyone.

How profiles work

Short to fill in, reviewed before it goes live

Every profile is added or changed through one short form, and TCRC approves each submission before it appears. Nobody needs a login.

Where possible

Title from your Tufts profile

If you have a Tufts faculty or staff profile, your title and department can come from it, so they stay current when your role changes. If you don't have one, you add them yourself.

In your words

The rest is yours to write

A one-line summary, keywords from a shared list, the questions you're working on, your partners, links and a photo. To change anything later, use the same form and pick your existing profile.

Before anything goes live

Approved by TCRC

New profiles and edits are reviewed before they appear, so no one can change someone else's listing. Community partners are named only with their sign-off.

Still to decide with CEPAR: the shared keyword list, and whether titles are pulled from Tufts profiles automatically.
