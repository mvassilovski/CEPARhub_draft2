CEPAR at Tufts University

Research done with communities, not about them.

A coalition of Tufts researchers advocating for inclusive, participatory and collaborative community research, advancing equity, inclusion and justice while producing rigorous, impactful scholarship.

What is Community-Engaged Participatory Research?

It builds on two older traditions, Participatory Action Research and Community-Based Participatory Research, and rests on four commitments.

Community-led

Community members lead across every phase of the research, not only the data collection.

Co-created

Knowledge is produced jointly by university researchers and the communities involved.

Action-oriented

The collaboration is meant to change something, not only to describe it.

Partnership-building

The work strengthens Tufts as a research partner communities can trust.

What the coalition is working toward

  • 01Reduce the bureaucratic obstacles participatory researchers hit, and expand university backing for this work
  • 02Increase training and opportunities for faculty and students across Tufts
  • 03Build more ethical, collaborative relationships between Tufts researchers and the communities they work with
