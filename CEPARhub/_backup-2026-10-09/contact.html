Which subcommittee handles what

Four standing subcommittees do the coalition's work. Route through TCRC and say which one you need.

IRB & ethics

Review problems, consent design, and protocols where the community is a partner rather than a subject.

Funding & finance

Grants, seed funding, and the mechanics of compensating community partners properly.

Community partnerships

Introductions, sustaining relationships between grants, and partnership agreements.

Campus scholar support

Training, mentorship, and recognition for faculty and students doing this work.

Add or update your profile

If you do community-engaged or participatory research at Tufts and you are not in the directory, that is the gap this site exists to close. Adding yourself takes about ten minutes, once.

You choose, field by field, what is public, what is Tufts-only, what is available by request, and what is not listed at all. Nothing about you goes public without your say-so, and the same applies to any community partner you name, their name appears only with their sign-off.
