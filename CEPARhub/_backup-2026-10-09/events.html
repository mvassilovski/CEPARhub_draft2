Sep 18, 2026Fall forum

2026 Fall CEPAR@Tufts Research Forum

A lightning round. Researchers give five-minute talks on a project they are working on, share what they are learning, and put a critical question to the room, the kind of question you would usually only ask a trusted colleague.

Open to anyone at Tufts doing, or curious about, community-engaged and participatory research. To present, send a short description of your project and the question you want to bring to the group.

RSVP for the Fall Research Forum, or write to tcrc@tufts.edu.

Why five minutes

The format is deliberate. Five minutes is too short to present findings properly, which is the point, it pushes people to say what they are stuck on instead of what they have already solved.

Most useful connections at past forums came from the question at the end of a talk, not the talk itself.
